Inguzanyo zihari

Inyungu ikurwa ako kanya igihe inguzanyo yemejwe (kuri rate yashyizweho muri Settings) igasigara mu kimina — umunyamuryango ahabwa ubwo net isigaye. Umunyamuryango ntiyemerewe gusaba indi nguzanyo mbere yo kurangiza kwishyura iyo afite. Umunyamuryango agomba kwishyura amafaranga yose yasabye (principal), atari net yahawe gusa. Uwarengeje ukwezi atarishyura, inyungu ikomeza kwiyongera buri kwezi (iziyongera ku nguzanyo, hanyuma ikabarwa indi nyungu mu kwezi gukurikiyeho), kugeza igihe azishyurira byose.

Amafaranga Agomba Kwishyurwa (hanze y'app)

Iyi ni urutonde rw'abemeje gukuramo amafaranga yabo yo kugabana (harimo n'abasohotse mu ikimina). Nyuma yo kubishyura mu ntoki, kanda "Yishyuwe".

Incamake ya buri munyamuryango

Igihe Admin atangije "round" y'ukugabana, umunyamuryango wese abona UMUGABANE WE WOSE (contribution + inyungu ye, hakurwaho ideni niba ahari) nk'icyo ategereje kwemeza. "Indangagaciro (byose)" ni gusa indangagaciro (estimate) y'icyo yagira byose iyo round yatangira ubu — ntabwo ari umubare wemejwe.

Saba Inguzanyo

Wemerewe gusaba inguzanyo kugeza kuri 0 Frw (bitewe ku mugabane wawe). Inyungu ikurwa ako kanya igihe usabye inguzanyo yemejwe — ni yo mpamvu amafaranga uzahabwa (net) aba make ugereranyije n'ayo wasabye. Ugomba kwishyura mu gihe cy'ukwezi kumwe uhereye igihe yemejwe. Utarishyura, inyungu izakomeza kwiyongera buri kwezi (ikaziyongera ku nguzanyo, hanyuma ikazabarwa inyungu nshya mu kwezi gukurikiyeho), kugeza igihe uzishyurira byose. Ntiwemerewe gusaba indi nguzanyo mbere yo kurangiza kwishyura iyo ufite.
